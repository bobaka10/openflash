Unlock your bootloader.

A locked bootloader refuses to install anything the phone's maker did not sign. Unlocking it is the first step of every ROM install. Pick your device, follow the steps, and let this page check and run the unlock for you.

Status: unlocking from the browser is tested and works on the Pixel 4. The Pixel 6a uses the same command but has not been tested yet. The Mi Pad 4 is explained step by step and its status can be checked here, but Xiaomi's own tool does the unlocking.

Read this first. Unlocking erases everything on the device: photos, apps, accounts. Make a backup first. It may void the warranty, and a few banking or payment apps can refuse to run afterwards. You do it once per device. New to the words? See Learn.

1. Pick your device

2. Google Pixel 4 (flame)

Google lets you unlock the Pixel with one command, and this page can send it for you. The only requirement is that the phone is not locked to a carrier.

  1. Turn on Developer options. Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Turn on OEM unlocking. Settings → System → Developer options → OEM unlocking. If it is greyed out, connect to Wi-Fi, sign in to your Google account and restart. Phones sold by a carrier (for example Verizon) usually cannot be unlocked.
  3. Open the bootloader. Switch the phone off, then hold Power + Volume Down until the bootloader screen appears. Connect the USB cable to the computer.
  4. Check the status. Click Check status below and choose the phone in the USB window. The page tells you if the phone is locked and if unlocking is allowed.
  5. Unlock. Tick the box and click Unlock bootloader. Now look at the phone: use the Volume keys to choose Unlock the bootloader and press Power to confirm.
  6. Wait. The phone erases itself and restarts, which can take a few minutes. It may show the bootloader screen or start Android. Both are fine. Go back to the bootloader (Power + Volume Down) and click Check status: it should say unlocked.
  7. Install a ROM. Continue on the Install page.

Check and unlock

Ready. Put the phone in the bootloader (Power + Volume Down) and connect it with a USB cable.
Prefer the terminal? The same commands 
adb reboot bootloader
fastboot flashing get_unlock_ability
fastboot flashing unlock
fastboot getvar unlocked

adb reboot bootloader needs USB debugging, or use Power + Volume Down instead. get_unlock_ability prints 1 when OEM unlocking is on. After flashing unlock confirm on the phone. getvar unlocked should print yes afterwards.

2. Google Pixel 6a (bluejay)

Same method as the other Pixels: one command, confirmed on the phone's screen. This page can send it for you. The phone must not be locked to a carrier. Not tested on a locked Pixel 6a yet.

  1. Turn on Developer options. Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Turn on OEM unlocking. Settings → System → Developer options → OEM unlocking. If it is greyed out, connect to Wi-Fi, sign in to your Google account and restart. Carrier-locked models usually cannot be unlocked.
  3. Open the bootloader. Switch the phone off, then hold Power + Volume Down until the bootloader screen appears. Connect the USB cable to the computer.
  4. Check the status. Click Check status below and choose the phone in the USB window.
  5. Unlock. Tick the box and click Unlock bootloader. On the phone use the Volume keys to choose Unlock the bootloader and press Power to confirm.
  6. Wait. The phone erases itself and restarts. Then return to the bootloader (Power + Volume Down) and click Check status: it should say unlocked.
  7. Install a ROM. Continue on the Install page.

2. Xiaomi Mi Pad 4 / 4 Plus (clover)

Xiaomi does not allow a one-command unlock. You need a Mi account and Xiaomi's own Mi Unlock tool, which asks Xiaomi's servers for permission. That is why this page cannot unlock the tablet for you, but it can check the result at the end.

  1. Make a Mi account. Create one at account.xiaomi.com and sign in on the tablet.
  2. Link the tablet to your account. On the tablet turn on Developer options (tap the MIUI version in About 7 times), then in Developer options turn on OEM unlocking and USB debugging and open Mi Unlock status → Add account and device. Menu names vary a little between MIUI versions.
  3. Wait for the timer. Xiaomi makes you wait before it allows the unlock. The time is set per device and is often several days. The Mi Unlock tool shows exactly how long.
  4. Get the Mi Unlock tool. Download it from Xiaomi's official page, en.miui.com/unlock. It runs on Windows. Check that page for Xiaomi's current rules.
  5. Open Fastboot mode. Switch the tablet off, then hold Power + Volume Down until the FASTBOOT screen appears. Connect it to the computer. If the tablet shows Press any key to shutdown, see the Mi Pad 4 tips.
  6. Unlock with the Mi Unlock tool. Sign in with the same Mi account and press Unlock. This erases the tablet.
  7. Check the result here. Return to Fastboot mode and click Check status below. Then continue on the Install page.
